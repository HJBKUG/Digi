Late Shri K.C. Garg

Founder, Trustee, and President – Seth Pokhar Mal Educational Society

A visionary educationist and an inspiring institution builder, Late Shri
K. C. Garg laid the foundation of the Seth Pokhar Mal Educational Society with an unwavering commitment to academic excellence, character building, and nation-building through quality education. As the Founder, Trustee, and President, he was the driving force behind the establishment and growth of the renowned Rukmini Devi group of institutions, transforming them into centres of excellence known for innovation, values, and holistic learning. His enduring legacy continues to inspire generations of educators and students, shaping young minds to become responsible, compassionate, and future-ready global citizens.

About Us

DIGILOGOUS 12.0 – SYNAPSE: Where Ideas Connect & Innovation Ignites is the flagship Inter-School Technology Symposium of Rukmini Devi Public School, Pitampura. It brings together young innovators from leading schools across Delhi-NCR to celebrate creativity, collaboration, and technological excellence. Featuring 14 exciting events across Artificial Intelligence, Robotics, Coding, STEM, Cyber Security, Data Analytics, Digital Media, and Emerging Technologies, the symposium provides students with a platform to showcase their talent, solve real-world challenges, and develop future-ready skills. With participation from 1,000+ students representing 50+ schools, DIGILOGOUS 12.0 continues to inspire the next generation of innovators, creators, and technology leaders.
