NeuroPitch – Ideas in Action

"Think Smart. Solve Real. Pitch Bold."

Event Details

  • Grades: IX-XII
  • Team Size: 2 students
  • Entries per School: Maximum 2 teams
  • Mode: Offline
  • Device Policy: BYOD

Overview

NeuroPitch – Ideas in Action is designed to encourage young innovators to think critically about real-life challenges and present practical, creative solutions.

Instructions

  • Identify a genuine problem
  • Propose an original and practical solution
  • Create a structured PowerPoint presentation
  • Deliver a clear pitch within 3 minutes

Judging Criteria

  • Problem Identification — 20 marks
  • Innovation & Creativity — 20 marks
  • Feasibility — 15 marks
  • Impact — 15 marks
  • Presentation — 20 marks
  • Visual Branding — 10 marks
