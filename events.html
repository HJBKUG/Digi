NeuroPitch – Ideas in Action

"Think Smart. Solve Real. Pitch Bold."

Event Details

  • Grades: IX-XII
  • Team Size: 2 students
  • Entries per School: Maximum 2 teams
  • Mode: Offline
  • Device Policy: BYOD (Bring Your Own Device) for presentation (Laptop/Pen drive backup recommended)

NeuroPitch

Ideas in Action is designed to encourage young innovators to think critically about real-life challenges and present practical, creative solutions. Participants will identify a meaningful problem from their surroundings—such as school life, community needs, environmental concerns, or social issues—and propose an original solution.
Teams will present their ideas in a startup-style pitch, supported by a structured PowerPoint presentation and optional visual branding elements such as logos, banners, posters, or simple prototypes.
The focus is on creativity, feasibility, and real-world impact.

General Instructions

  1. Identify a genuine problem from school, community, environment, or society
  2. Propose an original and practical solution (tech-based or non-tech)
  3. Explain how the solution works and who benefits
  4. Demonstrate feasibility and implementation plan
  5. Create a structured PowerPoint presentation
  6. Design supporting materials such as logo, banner, poster, tagline, or prototype (optional but encouraged)
  7. Deliver a clear pitch within the allotted time
  8. Answer judges’ questions confidently
  9. Presentation Duration: 3 minutes
  10. Followed by Q&A with judges
  11. All team members should be able to present

Judging Criteria

  • Problem Identification & Relevance — 20
  • Innovation & Creativity — 20
  • Feasibility & Practicality — 15
  • Impact and sustainability — 15
  • Presentation & Communication skills — 20
  • Visual Branding / Supporting Material — 10

General Guidelines:

  1. All ideas must be original and student-driven
  2. Solutions may include:
    1. Products
    2. Services
    3. Applications
    4. Awareness campaigns
    5. Systems or models
  3. The focus should be on real-world problems, not fictional scenarios
  4. Supporting visuals should enhance understanding, not distract from the idea
  5. Teams must ensure that content is concise and within the time limit
  6. Judges’ decisions will be final and binding

ReelForge

This event is a creative fusion of on-spot photography, visual storytelling, and AI-assisted editing — reflecting modern digital expression: Capture → Create → Amplify with AI

Participants will create a short reel/video from original photos captured during the event based on a theme provided on the spot.

They should:

  • Capture photographs within the school premises using a phone (nature, campus spaces, science areas, etc.)
  • Follow the theme announced at the venue
  • Edit the captured media on phone or laptop to create a short reel/video
  • Use AI tools for effects, captions, voiceover, music, or enhancement; however, any audio elements (rap, song, voiceover, etc.) must be AI-generated only.
  • Develop a clear storyline and social message
  • Produce a cohesive final video within the allotted time

All photos/videos must be captured during the event only. Pre-captured content is strictly not allowed.

Judging Criteria

  • Relevance to Theme — 20
  • Creativity & Originality — 20
  • Effective Use of AI Tools — 20
  • Storytelling & Message Clarity — 20
  • Editing Skills (Enhancement, not manipulation): 20 points

CodeSprint - Code Clash

"Debug. Solve. Conquer."

Event Details

  • Grades: 10-12
  • Team Size: 1 student
  • Mode: Online + Offline
  • Entries per school: Maximum 2Teams
  • Venue: Computer Lab
  • Languages/Tools: Basic Python

Overview

Teams will participate in a coding relay challenge where each member sequentially solves a part of a logical problem using basic Python libraries(random, numpy, csv).

Round 1: Online preliminary round (Hackathon)

  • Participants will solve real world problems using the Python language.
  • The round will be held online on the platform HackerRank
  • Participants will be selected on the basis of problems solved and use of better code practices.
  • Selected participants will appear for offline final round

Round 2: Offline Error Solving round (Debugging)

  • Selected participants will appear for a pen paper test.
  • Participants will be provided with a program with errors.
  • The marks will be provided on the basis of accuracy of solution.

Judging

  • Code Practices — 30
  • Correct Functionality of Final Program — 20
  • Efficiency & Use of Concepts — 10

Digital Dharma

Command & Conscience Arena

Event Details

  • Team Size: 2(1 from class group 7-8, 1 from class group 9-10)
  • Entries per School: 1 team
  • Mode: Offline Written + Oral Round

Digital Dharma

Command & Conscience Arena is a two-level team event for students of Grades 7–10 that blends AI skills with ethical thinking. Participants design effective AI prompts in Level 1 and analyze real-world AI ethics cases in Level 2. The event encourages clarity, critical thinking, and responsible use of artificial intelligence.

Level 1 — Prompt Engineering for Class 7-8

Participants will be given a detailed real-life situation or task scenario. They must:

  • -Analyze the given real-life scenario carefully
  • -Design a clear, structured AI prompt
  • -Ensure the prompt generates a practical, real-life project that can be implemented
  • -Include specific, precise instructions for accurate and relevant output
  • -Focus on precision, clarity, and relevance

Evaluation is based on the quality of the prompt written, along with the output.

Level 2 — AI Ethics Analysis for class 9-10

Participants will analyze a given AI-related case study and evaluate its ethical implications. They must identify:

  • Bias present in the system
  • Potential risks and negative impacts
  • Ethical concerns (fairness, privacy, misuse, etc.)
  • Practical suggestions for improvement
  • Participants will provide filtered solution after rectifying all bias

Evaluation is based on depth of analysis, ethical insight, and practicality of proposed solutions.

Synapse Street:

"Cyber Suraksha Natak"

Event Details

  • Grades: 5-12
  • Team Size: 8-10students
  • Entries per School: 1 team

The aim is to encourage awareness and understanding of cyber safety through storytelling and dramatics. Participants will present an original story centered on cyber security, digital safety, online threats, or responsible digital behavior, showcasing imagination, creativity, and communication skills. The students must prepare a Street Play based on the given themes

General Instructions:

  • The nukkad natak must revolve around cyber security and AI-related digital threats (scams, deepfakes, data misuse, etc.).

  • Use of simple props and placards allowed

Judging Criteria

  1. Creativity and storytelling (30)
  2. Acting and Dialogue Delivery (25)
  3. Relevance to the theme (25)
  4. Confidence (20)
