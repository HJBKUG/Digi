NeuroPitch – Ideas in Action

"Think Smart. Solve Real. Pitch Bold."

Event Details

  • Grades: IX - XII
  • Team Size: 2 Participants
  • Maximum Teams Allowed per School: 2 Teams
  • Mode: Offline
  • Device Policy: BYOD for presentation

NeuroPitch

Step into the ultimate launchpad for the changemakers of tomorrow!
The event challenges you to look closely at your surroundings—school, community, or environment—and identify the friction points that demand a solution. This isn't just about theory; it's about feasibility, real-world impact, and proving to our panel of judges that your team has the collective intelligence to create a better tomorrow. The future is waiting—are you ready to pitch it?

General Instructions

  1. Ideas must be original and student-driven.
  2. Solutions may include products, services, apps, campaigns, or prototypes.
  3. Emphasis is on solving real problems, not hypothetical concepts.
  4. Participants may create a structured PowerPoint presentation (optional). To be submitted before the event on the link provided by the host school team.
  5. Design supporting materials such as logo, banner, poster, tagline, or prototype (optional but encouraged).
  6. Devices (laptops/tablets/phones) may be brought by the participants, along with a stable internet connection.
  7. Deliver a clear pitch within 2 minutes. The pitch will be followed by questions and answers with the judges.
  8. All team members should be present at the event's venue.
  9. The judges' decision will be final.

Judging Criteria

  • Problem Identification and Relevance — 20 pts
  • Innovation and Creativity — 25 pts
  • Feasibility and Practicality — 20 pts
  • Potential Impact — 15 pts
  • Presentation and Communication — 10 pts
  • Visual Branding / Supporting Material — 10 pts

CodeSprint - Code Clash

"Debug. Solve. Conquer."

Event Details

  • Grades: X to XII
  • Team Size: 2 Participants
  • Maximum Teams Allowed per School: 2 Teams
  • Mode: Offline + Online
  • Languages/Tools: Basic Python

Overview

Step into a high-octane programming relay where individual logic transforms into collective triumph. The Participating teams will go through Round 1: a gritty, offline pen-and-paper debugging crucible where automated compilers can't save you. In Round 2, Shortlisted teams will enter the digital arena on HackerRank, leveraging Python and essential libraries like numpy, random, and csv to engineer clean, efficient solutions for real-world problems.

Round 1: Offline Error-Solving round (Debugging)

  • Participants' teams will appear for a pen and paper test.
  • Participants will be provided with a program with errors.
  • The marks will be provided on the basis of the accuracy of the solution.
  • Selected participants will appear for the final online round (to be conducted on premises)

Round 2: Online round (Hackathon) on premises

  • Participants will solve real-world problems using the Python language.
  • The round will be conducted at the school, and participants will be selected on the basis of problems solved and use of better coding practices.
  • Collaboration or code sharing is strictly prohibited.
  • The final decision of the judges and organizing committee is absolute, binding, and non-negotiable.

Judging Criteria

  • Logical Accuracy and Problem Solving — 30 pts
  • Code Practices — 30 pts
  • Correct Functionality of Final Program — 20 pts
  • Efficiency and Use of Concepts — 10 pts
  • Team Coordination — 10 pts
